1. Your account

Keycloak is the one login for everything here. You sign in once with your Keycloak account, and each app recognizes you. There are no separate passwords for each app.

Chris creates your account. You can't sign up on your own.

Before you start

Install an authenticator app on your phone. It shows a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds, and you'll need it to sign in. Any of these work:

  • Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator, free on the App Store and Google Play
  • On iPhone, the built-in Passwords app also works

If you already use one of these for another account, use the same app.

Set up your account

  1. Look for an email from CHILLINCOOL Keycloak (keycloak@chillincool.net). Check your spam folder if it isn't there.
  2. Open the link in the email. It only works for a limited time, so do this soon. If it has expired, ask Chris to send a new one.
  3. Choose a password.
  4. Keycloak shows a QR code. In your authenticator app, add a new account and scan the code. If you're setting up on your phone and can't scan your own screen, tap Unable to scan? and type the key it shows into the app instead.
  5. Type the 6-digit code from the app into Keycloak to confirm. You can leave the device name blank.

Signing in from now on

Whenever an app sends you to login.chillincool.net, enter your username or email and password, then the current 6-digit code from your authenticator app. Phone apps like Audiobookshelf stay signed in, so you won't do this often.

Managing your account: go to login.chillincool.net to change your password or authenticator app. You can also add a passkey there; signing in with a passkey skips the 6-digit code.

Forgot your password? Click Forgot Password? on the sign-in page and Keycloak will email you a reset link.

2. Audiobooks: Audiobookshelf

Works anywhere

On the web

  1. Go to audiobookshelf.chillincool.net.
  2. Click Login with Keycloak. Don't type into the username and password boxes above it; those are for a different kind of account and will say "Unauthorized".
  3. Sign in with your Keycloak account. You'll land in the library.

On your phone

iPhone: Plappa

Install Plappa from the App Store. It's free to listen. Downloading books for offline listening is a one-time $4.99 upgrade inside the app.

  1. Add a server and enter https://audiobookshelf.chillincool.net
  2. Choose the SSO / OpenID sign-in option, not username and password.
  3. Sign in with Keycloak when it opens the login page.

Android: Audiobookshelf

Install the official Audiobookshelf app from the Google Play Store. It's free, including downloads.

  1. Enter the server address https://audiobookshelf.chillincool.net
  2. Tap Login with Keycloak.
  3. Sign in with Keycloak when it opens the login page.

Your listening position syncs between the website and your phone, so you can pick up where you left off on either. Download books before a trip or a long drive in case you lose signal.

3. Ebooks: BookBoss

Works anywhere

Before your first visit, Chris needs to create your BookBoss account using the same email address as your Keycloak account. Ask Chris to set it up.

  1. Go to bookboss.chillincool.net.
  2. Click Sign in with Keycloak and sign in.
  3. Browse by title, author, or series. Open a book and download it.

Reading on a Kindle

  1. Download the book from BookBoss. It will be an EPUB file.
  2. Go to amazon.com/sendtokindle, sign in with your Amazon account, and upload the file. Amazon converts it and it appears on your Kindle.

You can also email the file to your Kindle's own email address, which you'll find in your Amazon account under Devices. Amazon only accepts email from addresses on your approved list, which is in the same place.

Other readers

Reading apps that support OPDS can connect to BookBoss directly. Your OPDS address and password are on your BookBoss profile page. Kobo e-readers can also sync from your profile page.

4. Requesting new books: Shelfmark

Works anywhere
  1. Go to shelfmark.chillincool.net and sign in with Keycloak.
  2. Search for a book, then choose whether you want the ebook or the audiobook.
  3. Pick a result and request it. For audiobooks, look for formats like m4b or mp3 and sizes in the hundreds of MB. Ebooks are EPUB files of a few MB.

Chris approves each request. Once it's approved and downloaded, audiobooks appear in Audiobookshelf and ebooks appear in BookBoss.

If something goes wrong

"Access denied" after signing in
Your account hasn't been given access to that app yet. Ask Chris.
"Unauthorized" in Audiobookshelf
You used the username and password boxes. Use the Login with Keycloak button instead.
The 6-digit code is rejected
Codes change every 30 seconds, so type the one currently showing. If it keeps failing, check your phone's date and time are set automatically.
New phone, or lost your authenticator app
Ask Chris to reset it. You'll set it up again the next time you sign in.
The setup email link doesn't work
It has expired. Ask Chris to send a new one.
BookBoss says no account exists
Chris needs to create your BookBoss account with your email first.
Questions? Ask Chris.
