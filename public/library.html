Your library, set up

1. Your account

Keycloak is the one login for everything here. You sign in once with your Keycloak account, and each app recognizes you. There are no separate passwords for each app.

Chris creates your account. You can't sign up on your own.

Before you start

Install an authenticator app on your phone. It shows a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds, and you'll need it to sign in. Any of these work:

  • Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator, free on the App Store and Google Play
  • On iPhone, the built-in Passwords app also works

If you already use one of these for another account, use the same app.

Set up your account

  1. Look for an email from CHILLINCOOL Keycloak (keycloak@chillincool.net). Check your spam folder if it isn't there.
  2. Open the link in the email. It only works for a limited time, so do this soon. If it has expired, ask Chris to send a new one.
  3. Choose a password.
  4. Keycloak shows a QR code. In your authenticator app, add a new account and scan the code. If you're setting up on your phone and can't scan your own screen, tap Unable to scan? and type the key it shows into the app instead.
  5. Type the 6-digit code from the app into Keycloak to confirm. You can leave the device name blank.

Signing in from now on

Whenever an app sends you to login.chillincool.net, enter your username or email and password, then the current 6-digit code from your authenticator app. The BookOrbit phone app stays signed in, so you won't do this often.

Managing your account: go to login.chillincool.net to change your password or authenticator app. You can also add a passkey there; signing in with a passkey skips the 6-digit code.

Forgot your password? Click Forgot Password? on the sign-in page and Keycloak will email you a reset link.

2. Getting into BookOrbit

Works anywhere

On the web

  1. Go to bookorbit.chillincool.net.
  2. Click Keycloak and sign in with your Keycloak account.
  3. You'll land in the library. Your BookOrbit account is created automatically the first time you sign in.

On your phone

Install the BookOrbit app from the App Store (iPhone) or the Google Play Store (Android).

  1. Enter the server address https://bookorbit.chillincool.net
  2. Choose Keycloak to sign in. There is no separate BookOrbit password.
  3. Sign in with Keycloak when it opens the login page.

The app may offer a paid Premium upgrade. You don't need it to read or listen; ask Chris if you're unsure.

3. Audiobooks

Works anywhere
  1. Open an audiobook in BookOrbit, on the web or in the app.
  2. Press play. On your phone it keeps playing in the background.

Your listening position syncs between the website and your phone, so you can pick up where you left off on either. Download books in the app before a trip or a long drive in case you lose signal.

4. Ebooks

Works anywhere

Browse by title, author, or series. Open a book to read it right in your browser or the phone app, or download the file to read somewhere else.

Reading on a Kindle

  1. Download the book from BookOrbit. It will be an EPUB file.
  2. Go to amazon.com/sendtokindle, sign in with your Amazon account, and upload the file. Amazon converts it and it appears on your Kindle.

You can also email the file to your Kindle's own email address, which you'll find in your Amazon account under Devices. Amazon only accepts email from addresses on your approved list, which is in the same place.

Reading on a Kobo

BookOrbit can send books to a Kobo e-reader and keep your place in sync. Setting it up takes a USB cable and a small settings change on the Kobo, so ask Chris to help the first time. It starts in BookOrbit under Settings > Kobo.

Other reading apps

Reading apps that support OPDS (like KOReader or Moon+ Reader) can connect to BookOrbit directly. In BookOrbit, go to Settings > OPDS, copy the address shown there, and create an OPDS username and password. That username and password are only for the reading app, not your Keycloak login.

5. Requesting new books

Works anywhere
  1. In BookOrbit, open Requests from the menu and go to Request a book.
  2. Search by title or author, then choose E-book or Audiobook. If you want both, make two requests.
  3. Press Request. If someone already asked for the same book, the button says Add me too, and you'll be notified along with them.

Chris approves each request. You can follow it under My requests, and once it's downloaded the book shows up in your BookOrbit library.

If something goes wrong

"Access denied" after signing in
Your account hasn't been given access to that app yet. Ask Chris.
No Requests in the BookOrbit menu
Your account hasn't been given permission to request books yet. Ask Chris.
The 6-digit code is rejected
Codes change every 30 seconds, so type the one currently showing. If it keeps failing, check your phone's date and time are set automatically.
New phone, or lost your authenticator app
Ask Chris to reset it. You'll set it up again the next time you sign in.
The setup email link doesn't work
It has expired. Ask Chris to send a new one.
The phone app can't reach the server
Check that https://bookorbit.chillincool.net opens in your phone's web browser, and that the address in the app is typed exactly the same.
Questions? Ask Chris.
